About

Hi! I am a predoctoral research fellow at Stanford, working with Professor John Donohue. I will be applying to PhD programs this fall.

I hold a Bachelor's in Economics and Mathematics with Highest Honors from Swarthmore College.

I am interested in public economics and political economy, with a focus on poverty and social safety nets. Questions that interest me tend to center on how the political landscape lawmakers face affects whose interests they support, how that support translates into program design, and how program design affects who is ultimately reached.
